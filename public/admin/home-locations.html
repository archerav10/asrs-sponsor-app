Home Locations

Admin Tools

The Liaison app checks that on-site visits happen at the home by comparing the phone's location with these coordinates. The easiest way to set them: stand at the home with your phone and tap Use my current location. Or, in Google Maps, right-click the house, click the coordinates to copy them, and paste them into Latitude and Longitude. The on-site radius defaults to 150 m (about 500 ft).
