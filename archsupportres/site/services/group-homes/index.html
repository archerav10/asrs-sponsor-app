Congregate Residential

Group Homes

Group Home residential services that meet the needs of adults with developmental disabilities — in a peaceful home that promotes inclusion, community access, fun, and learning.

Home Comforts

Every resident has their own bedroom.

Our residents enjoy modern amenities in all of our homes, including:

  • Private, single-occupancy bedrooms
  • Flat-screen TVs in living areas and bedrooms
  • Outdoor eating areas
  • Gaming systems
  • WiFi and popular streaming services
Round-the-Clock Care

Trained, compassionate staff.

Our staff provide round-the-clock support so every resident’s needs are always met. Services include:

  • Healthy, nutritious meals and snacks
  • Assistance with personal care
  • Exercise and motor-skills development
  • Medication administration support
  • Skill-building activities to increase independence
  • Access to community resources and recreation
