Our program

Assessment

Each resident receives an initial functional assessment that evaluates their strengths, challenges, and needs.

Individualized Service Planning

Following the assessment, each resident helps develop their own service plan with support from program staff.

Case Management

We make sure each resident’s physical, social, behavioral, educational, vocational, and psychological needs and preferences are identified and addressed through service coordination and regular feedback.

Life & Social Skills Training

Residents receive daily individual and group instruction toward the skills outlined in their service plan.

Recreation

Residents are exposed to a wide array of recreational activities chosen to build interests, knowledge, and social skills.

Sensory & Mental Stimulation

Activities designed to engage all five senses and keep minds active.

Family Involvement

Weekly activities encourage regular visits, shared fun, and consistent communication between staff, families, and authorized representatives.

Horticulture

Hands-on sessions in caring for plants, fruits, and vegetables — how to plant and harvest food and help keep a healthy environment.
