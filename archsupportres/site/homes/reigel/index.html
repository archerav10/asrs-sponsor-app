About This Home

Person-centered care, built around nature and family.

This Sponsored Residential home in Emporia provides a quiet, safe, and highly accessible setting for individuals with special needs to live comfortably while staying deeply connected to family. Sponsored residential care here blends professional support with an enriching, small-town lifestyle.

The sponsor follows a person-centered approach, working with the individual and their family to help build the life they want. When the weather permits, they take full advantage of everything nature has to offer.
