Privacy

Privacy Policy

Arch Support Residential Services (“we”) operates archsupportres.com and vagrouphomes.com. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you contact us, apply for a job, inquire about becoming a sponsor, or sign up for vacancy alerts, we collect the information you provide — such as your name, phone number, email address, location, and your answers to our questions.

How we use it

We use your information only to respond to you, to review employment and sponsor inquiries, and to send the messages you signed up for.

Text messages

If you sign up for vacancy alerts, message frequency varies (typically a few messages per month). Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to unsubscribe at any time, or HELP for help.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their marketing purposes. Mobile phone numbers and text-messaging consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates. We use service providers (such as our website host and messaging platform) only to operate our communications.
