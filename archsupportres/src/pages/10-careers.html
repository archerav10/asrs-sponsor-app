Direct Support Professional

Direct Support Professionals help the people we support live safe, healthy, and full lives at home and in the community. The role includes:

  • Monitoring and supporting individuals throughout the day
  • Daily living skills development
  • Assistance with bathing, toileting, and grooming
  • Preparing meals
  • Transportation to appointments and community activities
  • Administering medications (training provided)
What We Look For

Is this you?

  • Committed to high-quality care and a passion for serving others
  • A team player with a drive to grow and learn
  • Able to maintain confidentiality
  • Experience working with adults with special needs is a plus
  • A satisfactory driving record is a plus
