What we collect

When you sign up for vacancy alerts or contact us through this site, we collect the information you provide: your name, agency, area served, mobile phone number, email address, and contact preference.

We use your information only to send the messages you signed up for and to respond to your inquiries. Vacancy alerts consist of a message when a new opening becomes available and one monthly roundup of current openings.

Sharing

Mobile phone numbers and text-messaging consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates. We use service providers (such as our website host and messaging platform) only to operate our communications.
